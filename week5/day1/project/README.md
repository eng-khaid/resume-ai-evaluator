Reflex Pro & NEUROSTRIKE — Week 5 Day 1

Overview

This folder contains two small interactive reaction-test demos I made during Week 5 Day 1 of the bootcamp. They're lightweight, single-file demos that measure click/tap reaction times across a short series of rounds and show a simple results dashboard.

What's included

- `index.html` — Reflex Pro: polished UI, countdown, 5-round test, results, confetti.
- `index2.html` — NEUROSTRIKE: alternate skin with richer analytics and VFX.

Why I made this

I wanted to practice UI animation, timing accuracy with `performance.now()`, and simple client-side state management. These demos are focused on polish and small, reusable patterns for future game-like utilities.

How to run

- Open either `index.html` or `index2.html` in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
- No build step required — just open the file and click/tap inside the arena to start.

Notes

- High scores are stored in `localStorage` under `reflex_pro_best_avg` and `neurostrike_v2_best`.
- These are demo files — they intentionally avoid external build tools to stay simple.
